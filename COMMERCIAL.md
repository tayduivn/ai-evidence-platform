# Commercial Licensing & Enterprise Options

The **AI Evidence & Accountability Platform** is released under a **Dual-Licensing Model**:
1. **Open Source Edition**: Licensed under the **GNU Affero General Public License v3.0 (AGPL-3.0)**.
2. **Commercial Enterprise Edition**: Licensed under a direct commercial, proprietary agreement.

---

## 1. Why Dual-Licensing?

We believe in open, verifiable, and mathematically sound AI governance. Security and compliance software must never be an unauditable "black box." By releasing the full source code under **AGPL-3.0**, we guarantee:
* Complete transparency for forensic auditors and security researchers.
* Free access for developers, academics, and internal R&D projects.
* A fair reciprocity model: if you build upon our open-source work and deliver it as a network service, you must contribute your modifications back to the global commons.

However, we recognize that many enterprises and commercial software vendors have proprietary architectures and cannot disclose their internal proprietary code under the copyleft obligations of AGPL-3.0. For those organizations, we offer **Commercial Licenses**.
